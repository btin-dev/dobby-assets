The table below summarizes all the platforms known to provide Liquid-based services, with details of the features they provide. 

Features

  • Deposit (DPT): supports Liquid asset deposits to a wallet. Depending on the implementation, LBTC may be converted to BTC balances on arrival.
  • Withdraw (WD): supports Liquid asset withdrawals to third-party or personal Liquid wallet.
  • Asset Exchange (EX): supports the conversion of cryptocurrencies or fiat for Liquid assets, e.g., BTC for LBTC.
  • Lightning (LN): supports the conversion and swapping of bitcoin from Lightning to Liquid (LBTC) and vice versa.

Summary of Integrations

You can access the official site of each platform by clicking the link in the company's name. Unofficial guides are also available for some of the platforms in the Guides column.

For more information on fees associated with sourcing or converting to LBTC on certain platforms, use this guide, available on the official Liquid Blog.
